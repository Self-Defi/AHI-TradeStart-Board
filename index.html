Rotation rules

1. Only trained, qualified, available, 18+ participants can be considered.
2. Responses indicate interest—not guaranteed assignment.
3. Default to one trainee per job; additional openings require an explicit count in the job details.
4. Prioritize lowest completed-shift count.
4. Rotate ties by least recently assigned.
5. Repeat participants if no other qualified respondent is available.
6. Supervisor may decline a candidate for documented, job-related reasons.
